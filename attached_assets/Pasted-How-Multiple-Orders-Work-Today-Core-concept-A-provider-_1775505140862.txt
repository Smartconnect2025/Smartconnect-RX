How Multiple Orders Work Today
Core concept: A provider creates multiple prescriptions for the same patient in a single session. They are grouped together for one combined payment, but each prescription is submitted individually to the pharmacy.

The flow in detail:

Step 2 (Medication + Cart):

The provider fills out the medication form for one drug (name, dosage, form, quantity, SIG, price, etc.)
Instead of going straight to review, they click "Add to Cart"
The form resets, and a green-bordered Cart Panel appears showing the item
They fill in another medication and click "Add to Cart" again — the panel updates with 2 items
They can repeat this as many times as needed
Shipping and oversight fees are entered once — they're shared across all items, not per-item
When done, they click "Review N Prescriptions" to move to Step 3
Step 3 (Review & Submit):

All cart items are displayed for review along with the patient info
The submission loops through each cart item one at a time, calling POST /api/prescriptions/submit for each
Only the first item gets the shipping fee, oversight fees, and consultation reason attached — the rest get zeroes (this avoids double-charging)
A PDF is generated (or the uploaded one is used) and uploaded for each prescription individually
All the resulting prescription IDs are collected into an array
Step 4 (Collect Payment):

The URL contains all IDs: ?prescriptionIds=id1,id2,id3
The page loads all prescriptions from the database and shows an itemized order summary (each medication with its price, plus shared shipping/oversight totals)
The total cost is the sum of all medication prices + shipping + oversight
The provider chooses one payment method (Send Link or Charge Now) — the BillPatientModal receives all prescription IDs at once
One payment covers everything — the patient pays once for all medications
Key points:

Each prescription is its own database record with its own status, PDF, pharmacy assignment
Prescriptions can go to different pharmacies (each medication card in the cart stores its pharmacy info)
The cart lives entirely in sessionStorage (browser-side) — nothing is saved to the database until Step 3 submits
After payment, each prescription follows its own lifecycle (pharmacy submission, shipping tracking, etc.) independently
